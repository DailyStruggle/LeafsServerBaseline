# Vanilla Structure Coverage

Date: 2026-06-07 (updated: authored swamp-hut, desert-well, buried-treasure, shipwreck)

Tracks which vanilla (Java Edition 1.21) overworld structures the Iris pack ships a
jigsaw definition for, and which vanilla structures are still missing. Scope is the
custom Iris overworld only; Nether and End structures generate from vanilla worldgen
in their own dimensions (Iris does not govern them here) and are listed at the bottom
for completeness, not as gaps.

Source of truth for "implemented": files under `iris/pack-overlay/jigsaw-structures/`
and `iris/pack-base/jigsaw-structures/`. Custom (non-vanilla) structures the pack adds
on top of vanilla are listed separately and are not part of this coverage gap.

## Custom (non-vanilla) structures the pack adds

These are bespoke to this server and are intentionally not in the vanilla list:

`frost-caldera`, `frozen-adventurers-camp`, `soulforge-ruin`, `deep-forge`,
`buried-sand-temple-annex`, `geode-vault`, `prismatic-shrine`, `stoneborne-outpost`,
`toxic-prospectors-dig`, `mush-huts`.

> Note: `shipwreck`, `buried-treasure`, `swamp-hut` and `desert-well` are also bespoke
> hand-modelled assets (generated by `iris/scripts/build-vanilla-structures.py`), but
> they re-create vanilla structures one-for-one so they are listed as implemented
> vanilla coverage above rather than as custom additions.

## Out of scope (other dimensions)

Generated by vanilla worldgen in their own dimensions; not part of the Iris overworld
coverage gap. Listed only so nothing is mistaken for a missing overworld structure:

- Nether: nether fortress, bastion remnant, nether fossil, ruined portal (nether).
- End: end city (with end ship), the exit portal and end gateways (generated, not jigsaw).

## Priority for closing the gap

1. ~~Shipwreck~~ - done: `shipwreck` (tropical region, rarity 700), three vanilla shipwreck loot tables.
2. ~~Swamp hut~~ - done: `swamp-hut` (swamp region, rarity 700); swamp now has structure coverage.
3. ~~Buried treasure~~ - done: `buried-treasure` (tropical region, rarity 500), `chests/buried_treasure` loot.
4. ~~Desert well~~ - done: `desert-well` (hot region, rarity 500).
5. Badlands mineshaft variant - nice-to-have, lowest priority; not yet authored.

The four authored structures share one generator, `iris/scripts/build-vanilla-structures.py`
(diffable JSON sources under `iris/object-src/jigsaw/surface/`, compiled to
`iris/pack-overlay/objects/jigsaw/surface/*.iob`). Each has a jigsaw-piece under
`iris/pack-overlay/jigsaw-pieces/surface/` and a `maxDepth: 1` jigsaw-structure (no
`overrideYRange`, so they place at the terrain surface). Region placement is the same
per-region limitation noted for the cave set. Not yet deployed/boot-tested (server
boots are user-triggered).
