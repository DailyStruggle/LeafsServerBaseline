## How This Fits the Docs Workflow

World design sits between `design/` (technical specs and ADRs) and `usage/` (player guides).
Documents here are narrative - they tell the story of *why* decisions were made and *how*
the process unfolded, with links to the technical specs in `design/` for readers who want
the details.
